Study Identifier	OSD-984
Study Title	Low-dose Simulated Galactic Cosmic Radiation Exposure After Hindlimb Unloading Has Long-term Impact on Skeletal Muscle Metabolic Proteins
Study Description	Spaceflight stressors, including microgravity-induced unloading and galactic cosmic radiation (GCR), acutely disrupt mitochondrial function and contribute to skeletal muscle atrophy. The long-term recovery of muscle mitochondria following combined unloading and radiation exposure remains poorly understood. We investigated protein abundance changes 9-months after recovery from combined unloading and radiation exposure.

Study Protocol Description	"The Brookhaven National Laboratory Institutional Animal Care and Use Committees of (BNL; AUP-523) and the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS; AUP-4078) approved the parent study. The parent study protocol has been previously reported. Briefly, 6-month-old male and female C57Bl/6J mice (n equals 50/sex) arrived at the BNL. After acclimating for 1 week, animals were randomized to an absorbed dose of simGCRsim (0Gy, 0.5Gy, or 1.5Gy). Subgroups of mice for 0Gy and 0.5Gy (and all 1.5Gy animals) were hindlimb unloaded (HU) for 5 days before radiation exposure; for a total of 5 treatment groups (WB0, HU0, WB0.5, HU0.5, HU1.5). Animals received their randomized radiation dose over 7.92 hrs (0.5Gy) or 24 hrs (1.5Gy) or were placed in the beamline but not irradiated (0Gy), with animals in HU irradiated on the final day of unloading. One day after irradiation, HU animals were returned to weight-bearing (WB) conditions, and all animals were transported to UAMS. At UAMS, animals remained WB and were housed in standard vivarium cages for 9-months before euthanasia. Throughout the study, animals had ad libitum access to food and water.

NSRL GCR and SPE simulations are designed to approximate the chronic mixed field exposures in space by means of sequential acute exposures to a variety of ion/energy combinations.  The exposure protocols are on the NSRL website: https://www.bnl.gov/nsrl/userguide/simgcrsim.php (SimGCRSim).  The dose can be administered either in a single series of exposures lasting approximately an hour or in daily fractions over several weeks.  The GCR simulations are described in detail here: Simonsen LC, Slaba TC, Guida P, Rusek A (2020) NASA’s first ground-based Galactic Cosmic Ray Simulator: Enabling a new era in space radiobiology research. PLoS Biol 18(5): e3000669. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000669"	One day after irradiation, HU animals were returned to weight-bearing (WB) conditions, and all animals were transported to UAMS. At UAMS, animals remained WB and were housed in standard vivarium cages for 9-months before euthanasia. Throughout the study, animals had ad libitum access to food and water.	Animals were anesthetized with isoflurane until there were no longer pain reflexes and subsequently underwent thoracotomy. Five random samples were selected from each of the 5 groups (WB0, WB0.5, HU0, HU0.5, and HU1.5) of the female mice (n equals 25) to ensure statistical power and improve reliability of results. Skin was sprayed with 70% ETOH to reduce fur spray; skin was removed gently from the leg; using disenfected forceps and scissors, left gastrocnemius muscle was disconnected from the ankle and reflected back to the knee; The soleus muscle was removed from the now exposed underside of the gastronemius muscle; The gastrocnemius muscle was then carefully disconnected from the knee; the gastrocnemius was weighed after being blotted off; the sample was placed into a cryotube and then snap frozen in liquid nitrogen; Following random selection, samples were powderized using a biopulvarizer kept on liquid nitrogen; once pulvarized the sample was place back in a cryotube and returned to storage until shipping for analysis.	20-25mg of each powderized frozen sample were aliquoted and shipped on dry ice to the IDeA National Resource for Quantitative Proteomics at UAMS. Sample preparation and mass spectrometry were conducted according to the standard protocols of the IDeA National Resource for Quantitative Proteomics. Sample preparation included trypsin digestion of frozen tissue samples and extraction of proteins. The pooled controls are made by combining a few uL from each sample. They are run at the beginning middle and end of the experiment to check the technical variation.	Mass spectrometry was conducted using an Ultimate 3000 autosampler to pull up to 1ug of peptide sample and loading it onto a 0.5cm x 75um trapping column. Peptides were separated by an acetonitrile gradient on an analytical column, where the Exploris 480 (Orbitrap Exploris 480, Thermo Fisher) scans samples at 60,000 resolutions, followed by 50x 12 m/z DIA spectra.
